## Company under test
Foundry Build Co. — small commercial fit-out / general contractor, Austin TX, USD. ~5 staff, today on spreadsheets + texts + paper. Evaluating whether to pay for this tool.

Team (one login each — testing the multi-user promise):
- Owner/Principal (admin) — signs up, sets up company + people, approves money, reviews the business
- Project Manager (pm) — plans schedules, raises material needs, owns client change orders
- Procurement (buyer) — sources, compares quotes, raises POs/subcontracts
- Site Super / Stores (storekeeper) — receives deliveries, stock, allocates to jobs
- Bookkeeper (finance) — approvals, billing, payments, job costing

---

## ════════ VERDICT ════════

**Would Foundry pay for this? YES — once the currency bug is fixed.**

The product's central promise held up under a real multi-employee week: a material need entered ONCE by the PM on a task flowed, untouched, to the buyer's queue → RFQ/compare/award → owner approval → short delivery received by stores → stock + cost posted → task auto-unblocked → live job margin the owner read on Friday. Five people, five logins, one connected flow, zero spreadsheets/re-keying. That is the thing worth paying for vs WhatsApp+Excel, and it works. Job costing, RFQ comparison (multi-criteria, not just price), the approval threshold, partial-delivery + smart coverage math, task punch-list/comments, the client-ready invoice PDF, and the full audit trail are all genuinely strong.

The blocker to trust: the **Finance dashboard (and shared dashboard/action-queue formatter + invoice-create dialog) renders amounts in "AED"** for this USD company. Wrong currency on the money person's screen = quiet cancellation. Small fix, load-bearing for trust.
